About Me
======
Hello! I am Tianjiao Wang, a senior undergraduate student in Computer Science at Wenzhou-Kean University.

My research focuses on the intersection of Biomedical Data Science, Computational Biology, Medical Artificial Intelligence, and Machine Learning. I am particularly interested in developing data-driven approaches for healthcare analytics, medical image analysis, and computational modeling.

Currently, I am preparing for graduate study and research opportunities in Biomedical Data Science, Computational Biology, and related interdisciplinary fields.

Research Interests
======
My current research interests include:

- Biomedical Data Science
- Computational Biology
- Medical Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Health Informatics
- Medical Image Analysis
